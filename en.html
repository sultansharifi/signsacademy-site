Your child won’t just read the Quran — they’ll come to know God and themselves

Children growing up in the West ask big questions every day. We give them clear, sound, age-appropriate answers.

Knowing God

Coming to know God through reason, nature and the Quran — in simple language with examples from a child’s own life.

Questions answered in class:

  • Who is God, and where is He?
  • How do we know God exists?
  • Why do we pray and make dua?
  • Why did God create us?

Knowing Yourself

Your child learns to understand themselves, their feelings and their gifts — and to live confidently in society with a Muslim identity.

What they learn:

  • Who am I, and what is my purpose?
  • Muslim identity at school and in Western society
  • Managing anger, patience and good decisions
  • Good friendships and saying “no” to what’s wrong

Questions from parents

My child doesn’t understand Dari or Pashto well. Can they still join?

Yes. Teachers use simple language and explain words whenever needed.

How do the classes work?

Classes are live and online. All you need is a computer, tablet or phone with internet. We send the class link before each lesson.

When are classes held?

Class times are set in your local time zone, after school hours.

Do you have female teachers for girls?

Yes, we have both male and female teachers.

How are “Knowing God” and “Knowing Yourself” different from regular Islamic studies?

They answer a child’s deep questions about God, themselves and the purpose of life — something regular Quran classes don’t cover.
